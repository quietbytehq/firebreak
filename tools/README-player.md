# Firebreak 0.3.5 (Linux)

An Operation Wolf-style gallery shooter by QuietByte. Stage 1, River Village.

Run `./play.sh` in this folder. Godot is not required. OpenGL 3.3 graphics required.

Controls: mouse aims, left click or Ctrl fires (hold for automatic fire), R reloads, 1 / 2 / 3
or a click on the weapon bar selects pistol, primary or special, Q or the mouse wheel cycles
weapons, Space, right click or G fires the special (grenade or launcher), E or middle click
calls the air strike, Esc pauses, F11 toggles fullscreen. Arrow keys / WASD and a gamepad left
stick also move the crosshair (pad A fire, X reload, LB / Y next / previous weapon, B or LT
special, RB air strike, Start pause).
All controls can be remapped from the Controls screen.

Shoot soldiers, never villagers. Shoot falling food to heal and falling supplies for ammo,
grenades, weapon upgrades and cash. Kills earn cash; hitting a villager costs cash. Spend it in
the Store (title menu or after a mission) on new pistols, SMGs, rifles, launchers and ammo.
Scores, settings, cash and weapons are saved locally. A campaign score of 2,000 or more can be posted to the global top 100 at firebreak.quietbytehq.uk; press Tab on the name screen to keep it on this computer only.

The selected weapon is shown in Rayan's hands at the bottom of the screen. Pausing freezes all
game sound and plays a calm pause-menu track.
Soldiers and villagers have a thin white sticker outline. Magazine guns show a side-on pose
while reloading. Head shots award a CLEAN SHOT bonus.

Music, sound effects and voice are built in. To try replacement sounds, see audio/README.md.
