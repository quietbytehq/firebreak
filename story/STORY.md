# Firebreak — story

The opening briefing and travel map shipped in 0.3.6, as a plain mission to cut the Ember Ring. The award ceremony and stages 2–12 are still to come.
No real units, places, or trademarks.

## Premise

Rayan is a specialist with a small international unit whose job is to cut
supply lines wherever they flare up. The target of this campaign is a private
arms network known as the Ember Ring: smugglers who move weapons through
forgotten ports, desert crossings and quiet bays. Each stage is another link
in that chain. The campaign is the firebreak — stop the fire spreading.

The tone is pulpy and globetrotting rather than a single-year war diary.
River Village still has its golden-hour 1960s river setting; later stages
visit other theaters as the Ring flees and rebuilds.

## Opening briefing

Shown once, before Stage 1, on a dark briefing board over a painted map.
Short enough to read in about twenty seconds. The player can press on.

> RAYAN — FIREBREAK UNIT
>
> An arms network calling itself the Ember Ring has been moving weapons
> through river towns, desert tracks and quiet ports. They hide behind
> local gangs and leave civilians in the line of fire.
>
> Your orders are simple. Cut the chain. Spare the people who live along
> it. Do not let this fire jump to the next town.
>
> First link: a river village at golden hour. Lock and load.

A key on that screen skips to the title menu or straight into Stage 1
(to be decided at implementation).

## Travel map

Between stages, a painted world map shows the route as a scored line of
embers, with the next stage marked and a short line of briefing text.
The player confirms to continue, or visits the Store first. Score now
carries across the campaign (that work lives with the scoreboard).

## Twelve-stage route

1. **River Village** — A jungle river town at golden hour. The first
   staging post. (Playable now.)
2. **Dune Sea** — Open desert and rock canyons. Convoys crossing the sand.
   (Concept approved, not built.)
3. **Coral Coast** — A Caribbean bay at golden hour. Gangs on speedboats,
   piers and jeeps; civilians on the beach. (Concept approved, not built.)
4. **Iron Harbour** — A rusted industrial port in dirty weather. Cranes,
   freighters and warehouses.
5. **Night Market** — A dense old city after dark. Alleys, rooftops and
   lantern-lit stalls. Watch the civilians.
6. **High Pass** — A snowy mountain border. Switchbacks, a stone fort,
   a cableway.
7. **Broken Dam** — A flood-plain below a cracked dam. Catwalks, sluice
   gates and rushing water.
8. **Red Quarry** — An open-pit mine. Terraces, trucks and a processing
   shed.
9. **Blackout City** — A darkened coastal capital. Street grids, a
   blacked-out palace, tracer fire in the sky.
10. **Honour Gate** — The battle that earns the award: holding the
    ceremonial square and the gate of a freed city. The ceremony itself
    is an interlude after the stage, not a fight. See below.
11. **Storm Jetty** — A night raid on a hidden dock. The Ring strikes
    back after the medal.
12. **Ember Yard** — The network's last warehouse yard. Crates, rail
    sidings, a hard last stand. Campaign climax.

## Award interlude — after Stage 10

A short, playable-or-skippable scene on the travel map or its own still
plate. No shooting. About 30–40 seconds of text and voice (the new
award line, once it is approved and recorded).

**Setting.** A sunlit square behind Honour Gate. A modest honour guard
in generic dress uniforms, a flag without a real nation's marks, a
small crowd of civilians at a safe distance. Rayan stands in the
foreground in his usual kit, a little out of place.

**Script.**

NARRATOR (or a commanding officer, off-screen):
The Gate is held. The city is still standing. For that, the people here
asked that you be named.

(A simple medal is offered on a cloth. No real order or decoration.)

OFFICER:
Take it. You earned it. Then look at the horizon — the Ember Ring is
not finished.

RAYAN:
I'll wear it. Then we finish this.

The map lights the path to Storm Jetty. Stages 11 and 12 follow.

## Notes for later implementation

- Keep every name original. "Ember Ring", stage titles and the medal
  are working names and can change if the owner prefers others.
- Child characters stay out of the game, including the award crowd.
- The briefing, map and award can ship as still art, typography and
  voice over; they do not need a new gameplay mode.
